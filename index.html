Since 2015

A kitchen built around one fire, not six burners.

Olive & Ember started as a single wood-fired oven in a borrowed courtyard. Eleven years later, the oven is still the center of the room — everything else was built around it. Our chef, with two decades behind the line, treats the menu less like a list and more like a living document: it changes when the market does.

We buy from three local growers by name, not by invoice number, and we close the kitchen rather than serve something we wouldn't eat ourselves.

  • Ingredients sourced within 30 miles, daily
  • Twenty years of kitchen experience leading the line
  • A dining room built for long, unhurried tables
  • Private fire-side dining available on request
